Operator guide
  • One continuous flightMP4 or MOV, 1080p or 4K, up to 30 minutes, unedited.
  • The GPS log from the same flightDJI: turn on Video Subtitles (.SRT) or export the flight record (.csv). GPX and JSON also work.
