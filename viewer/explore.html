Project · single-pass drone survey

Zurich street corridor

A 10-minute drone video with ordinary GPS, reconstructed into a georeferenced 3D model on one GPU. Accuracy is measured against the Swiss national airborne laser survey, which the pipeline does not use.

Evidence

Speed, accuracy and coverage, measured against independent survey data

Model shape and coverage are scored against the Swiss national airborne laser survey (swisstopo swissSURFACE3D); camera positions against the dataset's reference trajectory. Neither reference is an input to the pipeline. A single evaluation-only rigid alignment separates model shape from GPS placement error.

Visible-scene coverage

Share of survey points the drone camera could see that have model surface within 1 m. Never-seen surfaces are not invented.
