One drone flight in.

This is a real 10-minute drone video flown once along city streets in Zurich, Switzerland (public research footage, ordinary GPS). VoxelFlight turned it into the 3D model behind this card on one GPU. Every number below is checked against Switzerland's national airborne laser survey, which the pipeline never sees.

Every number is checked against survey data the pipeline never sees.

Model shape and coverage are scored against Switzerland's national airborne laser survey (swisstopo swissSURFACE3D), camera positions against the dataset's reference trajectory. One evaluation-only alignment separates the model's shape from GPS placement error.
