# Demo

## Public interactive prototype

[Open VoxelFlight](https://voxelflight-3d.web.app/) and choose **Explore as a guest**. Reviewers do not need to sign in to inspect the saved flight.

1. **Watch the flight:** play the real 10-minute camera flight (4× time-lapse).
2. **Explore in 3D:** open the photo-textured surface, then try the camera presets, layers (measured surface, road-level gap fill, flight path) and measurement.
3. **Check the evidence:** speed, model shape against survey LiDAR, absolute placement (and RTK), and visible-scene coverage.
4. **Download:** open the output menu for model and evidence files.

Live uploads are offline during judging; the saved model was produced by the same pipeline in `vf2/`, whose run reports are in `vf2/RESULTS.md`.

[Real source-camera flight — 10 minutes as a 4× time-lapse](https://voxelflight-3d.web.app/assets/latest/flight-preview.mp4). This is the input camera sequence, **not a narrated product demonstration or a reconstruction render**.
