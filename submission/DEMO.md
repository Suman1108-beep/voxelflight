# Demo

## Public interactive prototype

[Open VoxelFlight](https://voxelflight-3d.web.app/) and choose **Explore as a guest**. Reviewers do not need to sign in to inspect the saved flight.

1. **Watch the flight:** play the real 60-second camera sequence.
2. **Explore in 3D:** open the actual exported surface, then try the camera and layer controls.
3. **Check the evidence:** compare absolute camera error with reference-aligned trajectory error and read the remaining validation gaps.
4. **Download:** open the output menu for model and evidence files.

New reconstruction is a separate, authenticated preview workflow and needs an available processing worker. The public saved demo is not a recording of a fresh end-to-end inference job.
